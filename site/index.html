    Method

    1. Backlog is each company's remaining performance obligations, taken as first reported in its quarterly or annual filing.
    2. The backlog model multiplies backlog by the rate at which past backlog turned into revenue.
    3. The baselines repeat last year's revenue: the same quarter for the next quarter, the trailing four quarters for the next four, each with and without trailing growth.
    4. Every forecast uses only figures known at its origin quarter and is scored by its absolute percentage error.
    5. The design was fixed in writing before any result existed.
